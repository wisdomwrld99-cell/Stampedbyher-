FAQ

Good to know

What should I bring?

A valid, government-issued photo ID for every signer, and the unsigned documents. Do not sign beforehand.

Can you notarize outside business hours?

Yes. We are available 24/7, subject to scheduling.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Notaries verify identity and witness signatures but cannot give legal advice or choose documents for you.

How is pricing determined?

It depends on the service, travel distance and time of day. Call or text for a quick quote.
